Global Politics Seminar Workbook

As part of our commitment to an "analog" classroom environment, this course requires you to complete all major recurring assignments by hand. The printable workbook contains dedicated sections for your Living Rights Portfolio reflections, your Debate Project worksheets, and notes for the Friday Case Study Debates.

Workbook Instructions

Please print out this workbook at the start of the semester and bring it to class. You will handwrite your assignments directly onto these lined pages and turn them in on the required due dates.
