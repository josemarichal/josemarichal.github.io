Synthetic Politics, Influencer "Slopaganda" & Democratic Trust

Auditing Partisan Creator AI Video Networks, Battleground Voter Polarization, Audio Forensics, and 28-State Disclaimer Legislation.

The Ecosystem Thesis: The danger of political generative AI is not just official campaign ads—it is the explosion of decentralized, partisan social media influencers and creator collectives publishing organic AI "slopaganda" outside formal FEC disclosure regimes. Our working group builds an interlocking public research ecosystem: advanced students harvest YouTube synthetic shorts and Reddit battleground voter debates, while qualitative and creative students investigate creator networks, produce forensic explainers, and publish voter literacy guides.

VOTER DELIBERATION ACTIVE RESEARCH

Battleground State Reddit Corpus (N=58)

~1,200 Voter Comments Across Swing State Subreddits Evaluating Epistemic Confusion and the Liar's Dividend.

TRACK: Track A (Computational NLP & Political Behavior)
RESEARCHER: Dr. Jose Marichal [Active / Published]
INPUTS: Arctic Shift API • State Subreddits (TX, GA, WI, OH)
STATUS: Live • Fully Published

When a deepfake video circulates in a swing state, how do voters actually react? By harvesting 58 election threads across subreddits like r/TexasPolitics, r/Georgia, and r/wisconsin, this project evaluates the psychological erosion of democratic trust. Findings show the "Liar's Dividend" in full effect: genuine embarrassing videos of politicians are now routinely dismissed by partisans as "obviously an AI deepfake."

OBSERVED VOTER BEHAVIOR PATTERN (BATTLEGROUND REDDIT): N=1,200 COMMENTS
EPISTEMIC_CYNICISM: "I don't even care if it's real or fake anymore, both sides lie anyway."

Finding: Synthetic political media degrades democratic participation not by deceiving voters into believing falsehoods, but by making voters distrust all authentic evidence.
