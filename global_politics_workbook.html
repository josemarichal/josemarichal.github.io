Part II: Debate Project

Structured empathetic argumentation worksheets leading up to your final debate performance.

Debate 1: Research & Claim Due: Feb 20
Task: Write your one-sentence debatable claim statement below. Then provide an annotated bibliography of 5 sources (3 supporting, 2 opposing).
Debate 2: Opposition Brief Due: Mar 27
Task: Argue AGAINST your original claim. Identify 3 strong opposing arguments with evidence. Write a reflection on what you learned from "steel-manning" the other side.
Debate 3: Argument Construction Due: Apr 17
Task: Build your affirmative case. Develop 2 main arguments with Warrants/Evidence. Address counterarguments and draft 2 questions to ask the opposition.
Debate 4: Outline Prep Due: May 1
Task: Draft the formal outline of your debate performance here. (You will transfer the key points to your 6 index cards for the actual debate).
Debate 5: Reflection
Task: Reflect on your group's final debate performance. How did the opposition challenge your views, and how equitably did your group participate?
