# California Lutheran University — Technology & Society Minor Course Inventory
**Source:** `courses-report.2026-11-11.csv` (Full Catalog Export, 5,367 rows)

---

## Executive Summary & Proposed Minor Architecture (20 Credits / 5 Courses)
After auditing every undergraduate department in the catalog export (`100–499` level), Cal Lutheran already has **64 undergraduate courses across 18 departments** that directly address technology's ethical, political, cultural, legal, environmental, or computational dimensions.

A **Technology and Society Minor (20 credits)** can be launched immediately using existing courses with zero new faculty lines required, structured around:
1. **Anchor / Core Requirement (8 credits — Choose 2):** Courses explicitly centered on the ethical, philosophical, and political dimensions of technology and data (`PHIL 350`, `PHIL 205`, `POLS 419`, `COMM 421`, `HNRS 323`, `DATA 350`, `COMM 406`).
2. **Interdisciplinary Tracks (12 credits — Choose 3 across at least two tracks):**
   - **Track A:** *Politics, Law, Surveillance & Digital Media*
   - **Track B:** *Ethics, Philosophy, Religion, Literature & Culture*
   - **Track C:** *Applied Data, Computing, Spatial Analysis & Digital Systems*
