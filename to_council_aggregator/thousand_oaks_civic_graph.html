Ordinance 1750-NS: Ordinance Number 1750-Ns: Municipal Code Amendment Ordinance / Municipal Code Amendment
▶ ⏱ 01:59:42
Introduces and enacts Ordinance No. 1750-NS, creating Section 8-2.06 and Title 9, Chapter 10, Article 9 of the Thousand Oaks Municipal Code to exempt qualifying deed-restricted affordable housing projects from specified municipal development impact fees. Presented by Cultural & Community Services Director Jonathan Syrett, TO Arts Executive Director Nikki Richardson, and Board Chair Al Lowe, the policy reform eliminates major upfront development cost hurdles, accelerating non-profit and workforce housing construction in Thousand Oaks.
⚖️ Passed Unanimously (5-0 Vote: Taylor, Adam, Newman, Gutierrez, Noonan)
