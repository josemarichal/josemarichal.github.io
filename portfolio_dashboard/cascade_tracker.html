Posture: Defensive Tilt — Trim High Beta, Hold Core Quality

High-yield credit spreads and sovereign debt metrics are flashing active repricing stress, and multiple EM currencies have breached crisis levels. Crucially, however, downstream forced-liquidation gates—US equity margin calls (SPY -7% / QQQ -10%) and indiscriminate flight-to-gold—have NOT triggered. Markets are experiencing selective risk repricing rather than an acute margin cascade. Notably, equity volatility (VIX 26.4) is elevated while Treasury market volatility (MOVE 98.8) remains calm (<100)—confirming that bond-market plumbing is still functioning and preventing a 2020-style dash-for-cash.

Credit Spreads (418 bps) sit above the 400 bps crisis mark, acting as the primary Stage 3 driver. Meanwhile, USD/KRW is at 1343.0 (4.2% buffer below 1400 crisis trigger), and US equity drawdowns (SPY -0.49%, QQQ -1.99%) remain well shy of the -7% / -10% margin-call triggers. Gold (-2.27% daily) and Bitcoin (-0.90% daily) show synchronized de-risking rather than panic flight-to-safety.
2. FX Carry & EM Contagion
USD/JPY at 159.35 keeps the yen carry trade in the elevated 152–160 watch zone, while the US Dollar (UUP -0.50%) is stable today, easing immediate dollar-squeeze velocity. Beneath the surface, EM FX contagion is active: INR, TRY, BRL, IDR trade past crisis thresholds (with MXN, CLP in stress), and EM equities are underperforming US stocks by -1.49% today.
3. Sovereign Debt, Volatility & Doom Loop
Long-end sovereign yields remain structurally heavy (US 10Y 4.92%, 30Y 5.29%; 10s-2s curve at +0.28%), with parallel pressure across JGBs, UK Gilts, and German Bunds. The Doom Loop Spread (10Y TIPS Real Yield 2.79% minus Real GDP 2.1%) is +0.69% — positive territory where real borrowing costs exceed real growth, compounding Federal Interest/GDP (3.94%).
4. Inflation, Labor & Real-Asset Pulse
CPI Inflation (3.7% YoY) paired with 4.1% Unemployment boxes the Federal Reserve between sticky above-target inflation and a softening >4.0% labor market. In commodities, WTI ($80.61) and Brent ($87.39) remain below the $110 energy-shock threshold, though sharp intraday moves in Natural Gas (+10.2%) and Silver (-1.4%) reflect elevated real-asset volatility.
