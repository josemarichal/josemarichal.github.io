1. Methodology

Variables coded across the 10 municipal cases and 6 public-sector AI governance domains
Step 1

Physical Telemetry Layers

Codes the physical and spatial data streams ingested by each municipal model, including LiDAR/3D meshes, satellite surface-temperature sensors, IoT air-quality monitors, traffic micro-simulations, and BIM models.

Field: physical_telemetry_layers
Step 2

Civic & Qualitative Data Streams

Codes the mechanism used to ingest resident input, including georeferenced comment pins, neighborhood vulnerability surveys, participatory budgeting linkages (e.g., Decidim), and VR/AR co-design workshops.

Field: participatory_civic_inputs
Step 3

Governance & Software Architecture

Classifies lead municipal agencies, research partners, open-source vs. proprietary licensing, and statutory or administrative safeguards governing data retention and algorithmic transparency.

Fields: lead_institutions, governance_safeguards
Step 4

Six Public Administration Domains

Catalogs peer-reviewed public administration and legal literature across 6 operational stages: (1) Vision Alignment, (2) Use-Case Intake, (3) Procurement Clauses, (4) Workforce Training, (5) Performance Metrics, and (6) Data Governance.

File: municipal_ai_governance_literature.md
