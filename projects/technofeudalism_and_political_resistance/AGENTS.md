## 1. Project Overview & Research Mission
Theorizing the transformation of digital capitalism into cloud-based technofeudalism. Analyzes how proprietary planetary compute and foundation models extract rent from civic institutions, while formulating strategies for political resistance: data cooperatives, public compute utilities, and open civic protocols.
