## 1. Project Overview & Research Mission
Investigating and prototyping Civic Digital Twins and participatory civic AI frameworks. Analyzes how virtual replicas of urban environments—fusing qualitative citizen input with geospatial and climate data—can serve as democratic digital commons for participatory planning, heat island mitigation, and community co-design.
