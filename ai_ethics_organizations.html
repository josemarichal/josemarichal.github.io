Data & Society

Algorithmic Justice
👤 Key Figures: danah boyd, Sareeta Amrute, Janet Haven

An independent non-profit research institute studying the social, cultural, and political impacts of data-centric and automated technologies, platform labor, algorithmic governance, and synthetic media ecosystems.

Signature Research: Empirical field studies on workplace algorithmic surveillance, disinformation vectors, and automated welfare distribution.
#DataStudies #LaborSurveillance #SociotechnicalSystems

Center for Humane Technology

Attention & Society
👤 Key Figures: Tristan Harris, Aza Raskin, Randima Fernando

Catalyzes the transition from extractive business models to humane technology. Dedicated to unmasking the "race to the bottom of the brainstem," cognitive security vulnerabilities, and generative epistemic degradation of democratic institutions.

Signature Research: The Social Dilemma documentary, Your Undivided Attention podcast, and course curriculum on Foundations of Humane Technology.
#AttentionEconomy #PersuasiveTech #EpistemicHealth
Visit Official Site →

Algorithmic Justice League (AJL)

Algorithmic Justice
👤 Key Figures: Joy Buolamwini (Founder)

An organization that combines art, research, and advocacy to illuminate the social implications and harms of artificial intelligence, with foundational work exposing gender and racial bias in biometric computer vision.

Signature Research: The landmark Gender Shades audit proving commercial facial recognition error rates on darker-skinned females exceeded 34% vs 0.8% on light males.
#FacialRecognition #CodedBias #BiometricAuditing
Visit Official Site →

Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR)

Algorithmic Justice
👤 Key Figures: Timnit Gebru, Milagros Miceli

An independent, community-rooted research space free from Big Tech corporate influence. Prioritizes the lived experiences of communities harmed by AI systems, documenting data worker exploitation in the Global South and resisting colonial computing hegemonies.

Signature Research: Fieldwork auditing sub-minimum-wage data annotators in Kenya and Latin America, and critiques of planetary-scale foundational model compute.
#DecolonialAI #DataWorkers #IndependentResearch
Visit Official Site →

AI Now Institute

Platform & Monopoly
👤 Key Figures: Amba Kak, Sarah Myers West (Co-founded by Meredith Whittaker & Kate Crawford)

Analyzes the concentrated power of the tech industry, illuminating how Big Tech's ownership of full-stack AI infrastructure (chips, cloud compute, data pipelines) restricts democratic sovereignty, market competition, and labor autonomy.

Signature Research: Annual landscape reports examining Big Tech cloud oligopolies, generative AI supply chain dependencies, and antitrust enforcement frameworks.
#Antitrust #CloudMonopoly #ComputePolitics

Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF)

Civil Liberties
👤 Key Figures: Cindy Cohn, Cory Doctorow, Matthew Guariglia

The premier non-profit civil liberties organization defending user rights in the digital world. Combines impact litigation, technical code auditing, and public policy activism to combat mass surveillance, automated policing, and warrantless tracking.

Signature Research: The Atlas of Surveillance database tracking police deployment of Flock ALPRs, facial recognition, drones, and acoustic gunshot detectors across US cities.
#SurveillanceWatch #FlockCameras #DigitalRights

Signature Research: Annual global league tables scoring microtask platforms (Remotasks, Appen, Amazon MTurk) and food delivery apps on labor transparency.

🎓 Pedagogical Integration & Course Research Frameworks

Students in Technology and Politics, Social Media and Politics, and the Civic AI Working Group can use this directory as an analytical springboard for empirical research papers, legislative policy briefs, and computational audits.

1. The Algorithmic Bias Audit

Contrast the computational benchmarking methodology of the Algorithmic Justice League (AJL) with the sociological, intersectional framework of C2i2 (Safiya Noble). How do technical error rate fixes differ from structural critiques of data capitalism?

2. The Labor & Material Supply Chain

Analyze how the AI Now Institute and The Fairwork Project re-center the invisible human workers (data annotators, content moderators) and planetary resources (water, energy) behind abstract foundation models.

3. Surveillance & Civil Liberties

Examine the EFF Atlas of Surveillance and Georgetown Law Privacy Center reports on automated license plate readers (Flock cameras) and facial recognition to formulate a municipal accountability ordinance for local city councils.
