Technology and Society Working Group

Civic Agency, Democratic Innovation & Critical Scrutiny

The Technology and Society Working Group is an interdisciplinary research hub dedicated to examining how artificial intelligence is restructuring social, economic, and political institutions. We operate at the vital intersection of constructive civic innovation and critical inquiry. On one hand, our research investigates how agentic AI and computational tools can be harnessed to promote the common good—empowering undergraduates and community partners to build living civic infrastructure, advance participatory governance, and deepen public policy deliberation. On the other hand, we actively develop critical frameworks addressing the systemic costs of generative AI: from the unsustainable water and energy consumption of planetary-scale data centers, to extractive data colonialism, algorithmic surveillance, and the erosion of democratic agency.

The Technology and Society Working Group is convened by Dr. Jose Marichal. Students and collaborators interested in joining can contact him at marichal@callutheran.edu.
