1. Theoretical Framework: Campus AI Growth Machines & Anti-Regimes

Extending urban regime theory (Clarence Stone) and growth-machine theory (Logan & Molotch) to higher education, this corpus treats Board of Trustees / Regents minutes as an observable institutional proxy for governing coalitions. Rather than relying solely on interviews, public board records capture formal resource commitments, degree launches, task forces, and shared-governance pushback.

2. Collection Methodology

Step 1 · 50-State Flagship Registry

Indexed all 50 state flagship public university governing boards in flagship_boards_50.csv. Where direct /meetings paths returned 404 or certificate errors, scrape_regents_ai.py walked parent paths and root-domain navigation links to locate official minutes and agenda archives.

Step 2 · Multi-Format PDF & HTML Extraction

Crawled up to 12 year/meeting subpages and 25 documents per board (1,066 total documents scanned), extracting text from HTML pages via BeautifulSoup and PDF packets (up to 80 pages/PDF) via pypdf.

Step 3 · Keyword Windowing

Matched AI terms (artificial intelligence, generative AI, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Anthropic, LLM, Turnitin, AI detection, AI task force, chief AI, machine learning) and extracted non-overlapping ±400/450-character context windows with source document URLs and year hints.

3. Corpus Coverage & Term Distribution

Out of 50 targeted boards, 47 resolved with HTTP 200 OK and 28 yielded AI-related snippets (381 total). The three boards not retrieved via automated HTTP were Illinois (403 WAF block), Virginia (403 bot protection), and Utah (ERR; minutes hosted on the JS-rendered Utah Public Notice Portal / BoardDocs).
