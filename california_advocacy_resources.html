California Advocacy Resources

Explore the leading guides, handbooks, and tools for effective advocacy and organizing.

16-Week Policy Advocacy Workshop Simulation

This hands-on, semester-long seminar explores the methods, tools, and skills used to conduct legislative and regulatory advocacy. Rather than just studying the process, you will actively engage in policymaking. For this semester, the simulation has been converted into a printable, physical workbook.

Workbook Instructions

You must select a specific California policy area to focus on for the semester. Each week, bring the physical workbook to class and submit your handwritten responses to the weekly prompt for a pass/fail grade.
