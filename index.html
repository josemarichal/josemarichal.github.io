PROFESSOR AUTHOR RESEARCHER

José Marichal

Professor of Political Science
California Lutheran University

I specialize in studying the role that algorithms and AI play in restructuring social and political institutions. I am the author of "You Must Become an Algorithmic Problem" (Bristol University Press, 2025). The book explores the unwritten social contract we have with the algorithms that shape what we see, hear and think.

My next project, "Machine Liberalism", looks at how algorithms and AI are changing our expectations of liberal democracy (forthcoming with Intellect Books/University of Chicago Press in 2027).
