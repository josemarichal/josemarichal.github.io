José Marichal

Professor of Political Science
California Lutheran University

"I specialize in studying the role that algorithms and AI play in restructuring social and political institutions. My work explores the unwritten social contract we have with the computational systems that shape our reality."

Author of "You Must Become an Algorithmic Problem" (Bristol University Press, 2025). My next project, "Machine Liberalism", looks at how algorithms and AI are changing our expectations of liberal democracy (forthcoming 2027).
