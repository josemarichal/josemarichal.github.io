Stakeholder Policy Analyst: N=500 Double-Blind Simulation

Research Question
How do different narrative and causal framing strategies (drawn from Deborah Stone’s Policy Paradox taxonomy) shift stakeholder alignment scores, Key Veto-Player neutrality (Weight 10 ≥ 40), and the weighted Political Power Index (PPI) in a simulated suburban municipal council hearing on terminating an Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) contract?

1. Methodology

Experimental design separating stakeholder construction (Sabatier's Advocacy Coalition Framework) from experimental treatments (Deborah Stone's Policy Paradox).

Step 1

Control Baseline

Loads a fixed municipal scenario (anonymized suburban city, Pop. ~125,000, MHI > $115,000; proposal to terminate a $275,000/yr ALPR contract and reallocate funds to radar speed trailers, crosswalk lighting, and evening transit) and 8 stakeholders (S1–S8) defined via ACF belief tiers (Deep Core, Policy Core, Secondary Instrumental Aspect). Baseline Weighted PPI = 46.26%.

Step 2

Experimental Treatments

Selects one of 10 narrative/causal treatments from Deborah Stone’s Policy Paradox across a balanced randomized schedule (50 replications per treatment = 500 runs). Provides only the unlabeled 100-word public-comment text, omitting theoretical labels.

Step 3

Double-Blind Evaluation

Evaluates the 8 frozen ACF stakeholder profiles against the unlabeled public-comment text to record each stakeholder’s post-treatment alignment score (0–100) and engaged ACF belief layer without access to the treatment ID or theoretical category.

Step 4

Outcome Calculation

Computes weighted PPI in Python: PPI = Σ(Alignment_i × Weight_i) / 49. Evaluates passage criteria: (1) Final PPI ≥ 60.0%, (2) all Weight-10 stakeholders (S1, S2, S3, S4) have Alignment ≥ 40, and (3) Council vote tally (S1, S2, S4, S5 ≥ 50) is at least 3–1.

2. Findings

Summary statistics by treatment strategy and mean post-treatment alignment scores across the 8 stakeholder personas. Click any row to view the exact unlabeled stimulus text.
