2. Zero-Bloat Streaming & Coding Methodology

Extracting national municipal deliberations from 5.1 GB of compressed Harvard Dataverse archives without local disk bloat
Stage 01 • LocalView V3 Filtering

2025–2026 Municipal Meeting Frame

Ingests Harvard Dataverse's meta_localview.parquet (305,024 total local government meetings) and isolates all 37,655 municipal council, county commission, and committee meetings occurring from January 2025 through April 2026.

meta_2025_plus.csv (n = 37,655 meetings)
Stage 02 • In-Memory Tar Streaming

Zero-Disk-Bloat Transcript Extraction

Streams all 3 multi-gigabyte Harvard Dataverse transcript tar archives (5.1 GB raw) directly in-memory via Python's tarfile.open(fileobj=..., mode="r|*"), extracting ±100-word context windows around flock, alpr, and license plate reader mentions.

stream_flock_extractor.py → 3,581 snippets
Stage 03 • Deborah Stone Coding

Symbolic Metaphor & Speaker Classification

Codes every deliberation snippet against Deborah Stone's 6 cognitive metaphor families (FORCE_MULTIPLIER_TOOL, VIRTUAL_MOAT_FORTRESS, SENSORY_ORGANISM, SLIPPERY_SLOPE_WEDGE, DIGITAL_DRAGNET, GUILT_PRESUMPTION) and speaker role cues.

classify_stone_metaphors.py → 124 metaphors
Stage 04 • Census ACS Integration

100% FIPS-to-Census Socioeconomic Linkage

Links all 270 municipal st_fips codes to U.S. Census Bureau ACS 5-Year summary files (B19013 Median Household Income, B01003 Population, B17001 Poverty Rate) with a 100.0% match rate.

fetch_census_income.py → 270 jurisdictions
